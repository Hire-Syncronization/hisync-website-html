Common questions about HISYNC

Straight answers on the products, pricing, security and how HISYNC sits next to an ERP.

What is HISYNC?

An enterprise software suite by Hire Synchronization Private Limited, built by ex-Big 4 consultants and Silicon Valley product leaders. Three products: HISYNC People (HR & payroll), AssetX (procurement, inventory, warehouse & assets) and Ordentra (operations, sales & point of sale).

Who is HISYNC for?

Enterprises, mid-market companies and growing businesses — whether you already run an ERP, use a few disconnected tools, or have no system at all. HISYNC fits every starting point, across manufacturing, retail, F&B, healthcare, services and more.

How is HISYNC priced?

One subscription per product — every module and feature included. No module-wise charges, no feature unlocking. See how it works →

Does HISYNC replace my ERP?

Either. HISYNC can run as your complete business system, or as a sub-system alongside your existing ERP and tools. Ready-to-use integrations include Microsoft 365, Google directory, Oracle NetSuite, E-Way Bill, e-invoicing, eTimeOffice, Razorpay, Shopify and KYB verification. See integrations →

Is HISYNC ISO 27001 certified?

Yes. HISYNC is ISO 27001:2022 certified by Ranalysis Certification Pvt. Ltd. (EGAC-accredited). Certificate 4DAFE675, valid to 14 May 2029. SOC 1 and SOC 2 are control-framework assessments, not a CPA attestation. Read the security section →

Is each customer on a dedicated instance?

Yes. Each customer runs an isolated application with its own database and domain. No shared schema, no pooled tenancy, no cross-customer query path. See the architecture →

Security, Compliance & Trust

Your auditors will ask. Here are the answers.

HISYNC runs payroll, asset and procurement data for Indian enterprises — on a dedicated instance per customer. This section is written for the people who have to sign off on that.

ISO 270012022Certified ISO 27001:2022 Certified ISMS

Certified by Ranalysis Certification Pvt. Ltd. (EGAC-accredited). Certificate 4DAFE675, valid to 14 May 2029.

SOC 1 + 22026Assessed SOC 1 & SOC 2 Framework assessed

Control-framework assessments by UK Certification & Inspection Ltd (2026) — not a CPA attestation. A CPA-firm SOC 2 program is underway.

DPDP2023Built-in DPDP Act 2023 Privacy in the product

PAN, Aadhaar, UAN and bank details encrypted at rest. Every decryption is logged. Data-subject requests and breach workflows ship in the product.

GDPREUAligned GDPR Aligned, not a seal

Where EU personal data is processed, access, correction and erasure rights apply. This is alignment with the regulation — not a third-party GDPR certificate.

One customer, one instance.

The controls below are product behaviour, not a policy PDF.

Dedicated single-tenant deployment

Each customer runs an isolated application with its own database and domain. No shared schema, no pooled tenancy, no cross-customer query path.

Encryption where it matters

PAN, Aadhaar, UAN and bank details are encrypted at rest with blind-index lookup, hidden from default API output, and every decryption is logged.

Tamper-evident by design

Approvals write to a SHA-256 hash chain. Depreciation ledgers are HMAC-signed. Tampering is detectable, not just discouraged.

Segregation of duties, enforced

Payroll maker, checker and disburser are separate roles. A database constraint blocks self-approval. Break-glass overrides need a second officer and expire on a timer.

Access control & SSO

Role-based access across permission modules, field-level security, and SSO via Microsoft Entra ID and Google (OAuth/OIDC) with directory sync.

Enterprise Audit Logs Role-Based Access Enterprise Approval Controls Maker-Checker Enablement Controlled Operational Workflows
